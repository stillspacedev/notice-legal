# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** January 7, 2026

Still Space Dev (Individual Developer) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Notice app (the “App”).

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, how it is handled, and your rights as a user.

---

## Overview

- The App is **local-first**.
- Journal data is stored **on your device**.
- iCloud Sync is **optional** and uses Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit).
- We do **not** collect analytics.
- We do **not** track users.
- We do **not** show ads.
- We do **not** sell or share personal data.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect:
- Personal information (such as name, email, or identifiers)
- Journal entries or written content
- Analytics or usage tracking data
- Advertising data
- Location data

---

## Journal Data

Journal entries and related content are stored locally on your device.

If you enable **iCloud Sync**, your data is synced through **Apple iCloud (CloudKit)** under your Apple ID. This allows your data to stay in sync across your devices.

We do not operate our own servers for storing journal content, and we do not access or read your journal entries.

---

## iCloud Sync (Optional)

iCloud Sync is optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time from within the App.

When enabled:
- Data is stored and synced by Apple iCloud
- Data is subject to Apple’s privacy and security practices

You can learn more about Apple’s privacy practices at:
https://www.apple.com/privacy/

---

## Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users aged **13 and older**.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or marketing services.

The only external services used are **Apple system services** required for the App to function, such as iCloud (if enabled).

---

## Data Security

We take reasonable care to protect your data by relying on:
- On-device storage
- Apple’s system security features
- Apple iCloud security for synced data

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
