## Common questions

**Is my journal private?**  
Yes. Your journal is stored on your device. We don’t read it, track you, or collect analytics. iCloud sync is optional.

**How do subscriptions work?**  
Subscriptions are optional and handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple ID settings.
